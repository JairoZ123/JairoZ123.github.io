Welcome to Midnight Club: Los Angeles

Midnight Club: Los Angeles is an open-world racing game developed by Rockstar San Diego and published by Rockstar Games. It puts players behind the wheel of an entirely explorable recreation of Los Angeles, free from menus, mission gates, or fixed circuits. The original game released for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in October 2008.

The Complete Edition, released in 2009, bundles the base game together with the South Central add-on content, including a free map expansion, extra missions, new vehicles, and a police car pack, all included on the disc.

Key Features

  • A fully open, explorable recreation of Los Angeles
  • 24-hour day/night cycle and dynamic weather
  • Over 60 licensed vehicles across five classes
  • Deep customization with paint, decals, and aftermarket parts
  • A licensed soundtrack with over 100 songs
  • Online multiplayer for up to 16 players
