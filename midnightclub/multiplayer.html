Online Multiplayer

Midnight Club: Los Angeles supports online cruising and racing for up to 16 players at once. Players can free-roam the city together, challenge each other to races on the fly, or queue up events created with the in-game Race Editor.

  • Race Editor: drop your own checkpoints anywhere on the map and share the result
  • Rockstar Games Social Club: online leaderboards and Rate My Ride photo galleries
  • Free-roam cruising with up to 16 players

Race Types

Races are not picked from a menu. Instead, players challenge each other directly, or pick up races through in-game contacts. The type of race changes the objective:

Race Types in Midnight Club: Los Angeles
Race TypeDescription
Point-to-PointGet from A to B first, with no fixed racing line.
CircuitLaps around a closed loop of checkpoints.
Time TrialRace the clock alone, chasing a target time.
Red LightStart from a dead stop at an intersection and beat your opponent to a set destination.
FreewayHigh-speed runs against an opponent along the freeway.
TournamentA string of races that unlocks a district or class champion.
WagerPut your own money, and sometimes your car, on the line.
Pink-SlipWinner takes the loser's car, with no do-overs.
